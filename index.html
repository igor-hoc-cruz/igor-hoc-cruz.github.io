LiveAI

Marketing operations run by AI agents, with a human holding the keys

More requests than a lean team could handle, and a real risk of AI acting on its own. I built the system that does the work and asks before it acts.

  1. A marketing request
  2. Routed to 1 of 13 areas
  3. A specialist agent does the work
  4. Human approval
  5. Publish, send or spend

The problem

Marketing requests kept piling up across ads, email, sites and reports, and handing work to AI risked it publishing, spending or changing data without anyone noticing.

What I did

  • Mapped the work into 13 areas, each with its own specialist instructions and knowledge base for the brand and for Zoho.
  • Required human approval before any outside action: publishing, spending, sending or changing data.
  • Gave every task concrete completion checks, so "done" always means verified.
  • Cut the context each request loads, which reduced token use per request by 60 to 87 percent.

What changed

Requests flow through one system with compliance rules applied by default, a full history of what was done, and nothing leaving the building without approval.

LiveAutomation

Ad leads that vanished before becoming deals

Half of one campaign’s leads were being dropped without a trace. I found the silent rejection and rebuilt the route around it.

  1. Meta lead form
  2. n8n route
  3. Phone already in the CRM?
  4. New: create the leadKnown: re-entry on the new product
  5. Any failure raises an alert

The problem

The CRM refused, with no warning, any lead whose phone number was already on file, so half the leads from one Meta campaign never turned into deals, and no one was told.

What I did

  • Traced the failed runs in n8n until the rejection rule showed up.
  • Rebuilt the route with duplicate handling and re-entry when the person asks about a new product.
  • Added alerts on every failure and moved all sources to the company’s official field standard.

What changed

The lost leads came back as deals, failures now raise an alert, and every source sends channel, UTMs and ad IDs.

How a project goes

The same six steps, whether it is a form or a whole system.

  1. 1

    Listen

    What do people need, and which data is missing?

  2. 2

    Map the data

    Where each field comes from and where it has to arrive.

  3. 3

    Build beside production

    New work runs next to what already works, never on top of it.

  4. 4

    Test

    Real cases, edge cases, and the failure path.

  5. 5

    Ship with checks

    Concrete completion checks, written before the work starts.

  6. 6

    Measure

    If it cannot be measured, it is not finished.

What I hold to

  • Understand the need first

    I start from what people need and which data is missing. The tool comes after.

  • Don’t break what works

    Big changes go in next to production, with a backup and a way back.

  • Done means checked

    Every delivery has concrete completion checks and only counts once it is tested.

  • Client data stays protected

    No personal data in code or reports, the least access an integration needs, and approval before acting.
